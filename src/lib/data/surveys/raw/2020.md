## Radio Stations Followed

### Radio Audience Shares by Half-Hour Slots

Audiences were registered for all half-hour slots. Before 5:00 audiences were minimal and less than 1% per half-hour slot. Audiences doubled from 1.69% at 5:00 to 3.58% at 5:30, again doubled by 6:00 (6.23%), and again by 7:00 (12.58%). Audiences steadily rose to 17.48% by 9:00 with this level being maintained till 11:30 (16.16%).

Audiences steadily declined in the early afternoon levelling and peaking to 9.43% at 15:00, following which audiences steadily declined to 4.23% at 19:00 and to 2.07% at 21:00. Night-time radio audiences after 23:00 were less than 1.0% of the total population.

### COVID-19 Note

Radio listening patterns changed. In previous assessments audiences rose sharply between 7:00 and 8:00; falling between 8:30 and 9:00; only to reach the highest peak at 10:00. In this assessment the highest peak was reached at 9:00 and was sustained till 11:30. Reduction in audiences during the afternoon and evening broadcasts was much more at a steady rate than in previous assessments. Night-time audiences were half of those attained in previous assessments.
